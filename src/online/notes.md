[query_encoding.py]: Converts questions into normalized E5 vectors.
[document_retrieval.py]: Retrieves and ranks passages using DiskANN and BM25.
[lexical.py]: Calculates BM25 scores and selects lexical candidates.
[answer_extraction.py]: Extracts an answer with its source, or abstains.
[qa.py]: Runs the question-to-answer pipeline.
[benchmark.py]: Measures answer quality, latency, score ranges, and reading coverage.
[tune.py]: Selects the dense-score weight using development questions.
